What it actually reads

Only hardware detail your IT team needs to plan repairs, replacements, and audits — nothing about how you use the machine.

Processor

Model, cores, threads, and clock speed.

Memory

Installed RAM, per-stick capacity and layout.

Storage

Display

Model, size and resolution, where reported.

Motherboard

Manufacturer, model and serial number.

Mouse & keyboard

Brand and model, where a wired peripheral is detected.

How it's built

Built to stay out of your way

  • Its own private keyEvery install gets a unique credential — nothing shared, nothing to leak across machines.
  • Verified before it runsEvery update is checked against a published checksum before it's ever installed.
  • No direct database accessThe agent only ever talks to the sync service — never your company's database.
  • Works offlineKeeps a local copy and syncs the moment you're back on the network.
